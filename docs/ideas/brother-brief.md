What Aaron's brother asked for, from his texts (2026-11-07). This is the core spec for the site.
Butterman and the idle animations are Aaron's additions on top: see butterman.md.

## His words (lightly cleaned)
- "Could you make a single page site of this little cat character."
- "I'm imagining him just floating in the middle of the screen. You can swipe on mobile or click and drag on desktop and it rotates him around on pretty much any axis."

## What that means for the build
- **One page, the cat is the page.** The cat floats centered on screen. The current "shelf of trinkets" home page gets replaced by this; extra gags become Easter eggs on the cat page.
- **Rotation: left and right only.** Aaron changed this (2026-11-07, decision e1107-36): drag or swipe spins him like a turntable around the vertical axis, all the way around, with no tumbling. Works with touch on phones.
- **Click/tap = random sound.** A pool of sound effects; each click plays one (likely with a little reaction animation).
- **Social links footer.** Which accounts: still to get from the brothers.
- **Brainrot features.** Open-ended: Butterman is the first.
