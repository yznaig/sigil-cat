# Gags, round 2: things that happen, and what they leave behind
Brainstorm for the revamp (2026-11-09), in the register of the bolt and the loaf + star blast-off. Nothing here is built.
Aaron picks; combos come out of the gags, not the other way round. Decision record: e1109-12.

## The idea behind the list
Every gag has three parts: a **trigger** the visitor finds, a **show**, and a **keepsake** that stays on their page
and can itself be played with. Keepsakes pile up, so a page that has been played with looks different from a new one.
Keepsakes also react to each other and to the eggs that already exist, which is where combos come from.

Sigil shapes are a budget: each new one has to be told apart from B, the circle, the zig-zag, the star and the loaf's
half circle by the one-stroke recognizer. Gags that need no sigil are marked.

## Combos that fall out
- loaf + star: the blast-off (already in the spec, N12).
- butter + loaf: the paradox (1). butter + O: the fling (2). butter + bolt: melted butter (10).
- bolt + bread: toast, which with butter is the paradox again, hotter.
- bolt + puddle: the shock reaches him through the water (3, 10).
- eyes + anything: the bread stares; the cloud looks sad when it rains; Butterman walks off wearing them (5, 14).
- box + galaxy: the box is what comes back from space, not him. Tap it.
- hat + butter + box: all three stack. The checks should hold him to it.
- galaxy + any keepsake: the ring (11).
