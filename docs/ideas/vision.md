# Vision: the mental frame for every choice
Aaron, 2026-11-07. Read this before any design or build decision on Sigil Cat.
When two options both work, pick the one that serves this frame.

- **A fun gadget website *experience*.** Not just a trinket to poke at: an experience you move through, with
  surprises that unfold the longer you play, and something you show a friend.
- **The olden days are our predecessors.** It descends from the classic novelty web (zombo.com, hampster dance,
  1999 home pages) and 1930s rubber-hose cartoons. Borrow their spirit: one silly idea, done with total commitment.
- **The goal is a modern art piece.** Old-web soul, modern craft: smooth 3D, careful timing and sound, polish in the
  details. Weird on purpose, never sloppy. Every gag lands cleanly.
